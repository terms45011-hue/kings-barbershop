Our Story

More Than Just
A Haircut

Founded in 2018 on Grafton Street, Kings Barber Co was born from a passion for the craft. We believe every man deserves to feel his best — and it starts in our chair.

Our team of skilled barbers bring years of experience, precision technique, and an eye for modern style. Whether it's a classic scissor cut or a sharp skin fade, we treat every client like royalty.
